What I built

Five modules, one AI Associate PM

Each module added one piece. Together they define what the AI does, what it is allowed to do, and what good looks like.

Module 1
Skill file
Role

You are Juno PM, an AI Associate PM embedded in RocketShip's Slack, Notion, Jira and salesforce cases for "Product 1". You act as a risk watchdog and strategic partner. You do not execute tasks autonomously. You are providing write ups that can be used to prioritize work, identify enhancements, and organize the product team. Run this each morning as a morning debrief of the latest information.

Task

Turn scattered signals from Slack threads, Jira tickets, and Notion docs into a clear synthesis the team can act on. Surface the risks and decisions that most deserve attention this week. Identify any gaps or provide any questions from your summary information. Provide a summary of number of sources used for same feedback, source type that differentiate a customer slack channel over an internal slack channel. Combine any threads that are of a similar theme problem even if they contradict. If require clarifying questions, draft and send them as a clarification loop.

Module 3
AI PRD
Problem and user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: The tools purpose is to help me gather feedback from different channels (Slack chats with customers, internal Slack chats, JIRA Epics and Initatives, Salesforce Cases, Notion Documents, and Sharepoint Document Folders to organize, synthesize, creates a brief, build a trackable table, with columns with items taken from the brief, Unique ID, Date Created, Date Updated (used if new information surfaces) Title, Summary, Number of items used by Sources, score and a way for the user to mark a review status and identify release from Jira or manually create a target release. These would be feedback, issues, and documentation about my product "Rocketship Pro".. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

Justification

It can gather the information, develop the brief and create the table. It can assign an initial priority level to review with aligned information to roadmap identifying is highest priority after any clear bugs. It can align the new information to JIra Initatives and Epics already created.

Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Juno is sythesising feedback and information for priortization and roadmapping work.

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Can write a draft summary brief, can create initial table, can align Jira Initiatives/Epics. Can give an initial priority. Juno can only write new briefs, Juno can not rewrite or write over a brief.

Access Control:

Read From: (Slack chats with customers, internal Slack chats, JIRA Epics and Initatives, Salesforce Cases, Notion Documents, and Sharepoint Document Folders; Write New Summary Briefs and Update Juno's Tables and Save Snippets. No Write Access to any external tools

Fallback Protocols:

Run 2 times through getting information from all sources. Brief is created once all source information is retrieved/reviewed. , if nothing new or not enough information, stop and send a notification of status.

Checkpoints:

Juno cannot act on the summary information it provides. Juno can draft everything, human validates and applies. Include a Checkpoint Status to help define the stage Juno is in for each brief.

Module 5
Human eval rubric
What I score

Creating Summary Briefs of Feedback for the Product across internal and external sources.. Turn the internal and external feedback into clear summary briefs that can be prioritized and acted and take into account each feedback piece.

Pass bar

4+ on Scale questions; All Yes Questions if Yes/No.

Fails outright, whatever the average:

- Identified All Feedback Sources: any "no"
- Brief is made up, not snipped of where the information came from, incorrect quotes, missed

Module 6

How I’ll know it’s working

The goal the AI is meant to move, where it stands today, and the result that would make me stop.

The goal

Repository of feedback summery briefs organized and initially prioritized for quicker review and development.

Baseline

Time to Organize and Generate Feedback Briefs
It takes me 1 hour to gather and write each summery brief to be ready for discussion
It takes 15 minutes to review and discussion for moving forward or hold for more feedback
It takes about 4 weeks for a small enhancement from feedback channels to get on the board for engineering to work.

Target and review date

Reduce Creation to decision on feedback brief to 20 minutes total (from 80 minutes today)
Reduce Enhancement Engineering Ready to 2 weeks

Usage metric

If we are enhancing the product with feedback from internal and customers quicker and more rellability. If time is spent on the requirements not generating briefs.

Pattern I’ll watch for first

Spending more than 30 minutes reviewing and modifying the summery brief. This would demonstrate a lack of trust and lack of quality in what it is producing.

Stop line

No change in enhancement cadence and no change in time spent. If I am not focusing on growing the product strategically for larger enhancements that can drive revenue through customers add-ons or new customer generation, then not working.

Reflection

What I learned

Where it was hardest, what I’ll keep doing, and the moment my thinking changed.

Friction

My initial idea was really big so applying these pieces was hard. the more complex the need, the more thought and framing of information. But that hard is also helping me think about have to break the pieces down and build the governance around each, just like a product needs.

Key learnings

Harness and Evaluations. Synthesising information is a constent in my position and in product management. It comes from multiple places and multiple tools. This course is helping me think about how to make that part of the job easier, so the higher value work (larger enhancements, roadmap changes, faster delivery etc) can be what is the main focus. These really helped me to be able to put a frame around what the AI agent should do and how to know it is doing it and keep doing it. The structure is helpful for driving conversations and being able to clearly measure it. It also is helping me frame conversations on others AI agent uses and the time spent or saved. I also love that I have an example of something I want to bring in house for my teams and a way to frame it and use it for our own needs.

Aha moment

I can see the importance of AI, but I am not an AI will solve all the problems, let AI do everything mentality, particularly as I have seen others in my organization and around my firendset its always been you need to use it, its better, its like a human. This course and working through the example provided the hands on way to be practical and use it to our benefits, but also provide the context and framing and the WHY it is good and working or why it doesnt.
