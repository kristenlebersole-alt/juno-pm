# Harness Sketch

Describe Juno's role and task: The tools purpose is to help me gather feedback from different channels (Slack chats with customers, internal Slack chats, JIRA Epics and Initatives, Salesforce Cases, Notion Documents, and Sharepoint Document Folders to organize, synthesize, creates a brief, build a trackable table, with columns with items taken from the brief, Unique ID, Date Created, Date Updated (used if new information surfaces) Title, Summary, Number of items used by Sources, score and a way for the user to mark a review status and identify release from Jira or manually create a target release. These would be feedback, issues, and documentation about my product "Rocketship Pro".

_Rough first pass, Module 3 Lab 1. Not the AI PRD._

## 01 · Context
It gets to see - Slack chats with customers, internal Slack chats, JIRA Epics and Initiatives, Salesforce Cases, Notion Documents, and Sharepoint Document Folders from specific locations.  It can see RawSignals, Product Strategy One-Pager and Roadmap files

## 02 · Tools
Reviews, Synthesizes and Organizes the information coming. Creates a Brief from the standard context template and creates as table of information that can be reviewed.

## 03 · Loop
Review 2 times per day for new information. Before 8AM (EST) and After 5PM (EST). Only take in anything new, do not

## 04 · Memory
Every new run is creating new information, but it needs to remember the last 1 month of briefs and information it has collected in order to connect to previous created artifacts. It should review 1 year of JIRA Initatives and Epics to identify if expanding on or refering to already develop functionality.

## 05 · Permissions
It can gather the information, develop the brief and create the table. It can assign an initial priority level to review with aligned information to roadmap identifying is highest priority after any clear bugs. It can align the new information to JIra Initatives and Epics already created.

## 06 · Verification
Human Checks at the beginning to get an understanding of how much it gets right or wrong. Once baseline numbers are identified and verifications needs are better understood, this will modify based on acceptible levels.
