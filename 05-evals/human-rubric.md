## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Creating Summary Briefs of Feedback for the Product across internal and external sources.
- **Value proposition:** Turn the internal and external feedback into clear summary briefs that can be prioritized and acted and take into account each feedback piece.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy of Problem Identification | Every summary names the customers main problem and provides the correct snippet accurately | 1 to 5 | Invented Answers; Incorrect Information; Wrong snippets of where the information comes from. | Title and Summary matches, but doesnt accurtately identify when clarification is needed and isnt something I would provide and show to the rest of the team for evaluation | Title and Summary matches what the problem from the set of information is. Clear information with connection to the source data identified. All pieces of information is available. Asking for additional research is clear. |
| Prioritization | Every items has a priority score and its following the priority criteria | Yes / No | No Prioritization number given.  | n/a | Priority falls in the correct area based on the score. It is given and is directionally correct based on the algorithm. |
| Identified All Feedback Sources (deal-breaker) | Is all feedback source information used | Yes / No | A missed feedback source. Juno needs to identify if it did not find a source relevant, but not dismiss everything | n/a | All briefs can identify the sources and no source is missed. A Snippet from each piece of feedback |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 25% of all Summery Briefs each Week
- **Who scores them with you:** Me and another member on my team
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** Rewrite the summary. if our scores are different. This includes the summary brief and prioritization score within the table. Review and count of number of feedback pieces.

## Pass bar

4+ on Scale questions; All Yes Questions if Yes/No.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Identified All Feedback Sources: any "no"
- Brief is made up, not snipped of where the information came from, incorrect quotes, missed
